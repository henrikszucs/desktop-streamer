## How to sign

Open a pull request as usual. When you submit your first Contribution, add the
following statement as a comment on the pull request, filling in your details:

```
I have read the Contributor License Agreement in CLA.md and I agree to its
terms for this and all future Contributions to the Project.

Full name:  <your full legal name>
GitHub:     @<your github username>
Email:      <your email address>
Date:       <YYYY-MM-DD>
```

If you are contributing on behalf of a company or other legal entity, the
statement must be made by a person authorized to bind that entity, and should
name the entity:

```
Entity:     <legal name of the company or organization>
Signed by:  <your full legal name>, <your role>
GitHub:     @<your github username>
Email:      <your work email address>
Date:       <YYYY-MM-DD>
```

Your agreement is recorded in the pull request and applies to all of your future
Contributions. You only need to sign once.
